    About cpo.today

    cpo.today puts every public EV charge point from official national registries on one open map and refreshes it every 10 minutes. It exists so that operators, investors, fleets, planners and drivers can see, compare and analyse charging networks from a true signal.

    Where the data comes from

    Everything shown here is pulled, unchanged, from official public sources: the national charging registries and national access points that operators are legally required to report to (for Greece, MYFAH of the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport; for Lithuania, Via Lietuva; for the Netherlands, NDW; for France, the national IRVE base). The Coverage tab lists every source and its status.

    Accuracy and responsibility

    We collect this data every 10 minutes for statistical and analytical purposes only. We do not verify, correct or edit it. If a location, status, price or any other detail is wrong, the error is in what the operator reported to the registry: please contact the operator or the responsible authority directly. cpo.today accepts no responsibility for the accuracy, completeness or timeliness of registry data, or for decisions taken on the basis of it.

    Prices

    Prices are the ad-hoc tariffs operators publish in the registry, in the registry's currency. The per-kWh figure is the energy component of the tariff attached to each connector; an operator's "€/kWh" in the table is the median across its priced connectors. Session fees, time-based and parking components are shown on the location card when published. Many connectors publish no tariff at all.
