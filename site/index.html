About cpo.today

cpo.today puts every public EV charge point from official national registries on one open map and refreshes it every 10 minutes. It exists so that operators, investors, fleets, planners and drivers can see, compare and analyse charging networks from a true signal rather than marketing claims.

Where the data comes from

Greece: MYFAH, the registry of charging infrastructure and e-mobility market operators run by the Hellenic Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport. Operators are legally required to report their locations and live status there.

How it works

A scheduled job downloads the registry files, validates them, normalises them into a compact open format and publishes them to a public GitHub branch. This site reads only those published files. Nothing is edited by hand and no data is stored about you.

Open data

All normalised data is available as JSON under /data/, including 10-minute history and status-change events. See the repository for the schema. Free to use with attribution to cpo.today and the original registry.
